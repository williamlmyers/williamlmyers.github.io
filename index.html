About William L. Myers

Bill is a seasoned professional offering over thirty years of experience in Information Technology with a unique combination of experiences and talents. A proven leader who capitalizes on team dynamics, strong partnerships, and creative thinking to deliver quality results, Bill continually analyzes strategies and measures performance for long-term business improvement and success.

As a broad-minded quick study with extensive involvement in many domains, Bill has always found it fulfilling to help others and to lend his talents to furthering other peoples' visions and dreams. Empathy is at the core of everything Bill does.

Based on strong technological skills, business acumen, project management, and leadership, his career progression has been characterized by senior leadership roles spanning mainframes, personal computers, networks, applications, relational databases, the Internet, customer support, and high-performance building construction. Bill is a strong family man with two children, and he has been with his wife for over 35 years.

A Serial "Entrepre-doer" and Builder, Bill has been involved in many start-ups and businesses. From Information Technology to Construction to Business Management to Philanthropy, Bill is always looking for that next "Shark Tank" idea and opportunity. He has worked in a large array of endeavors and projects, many of them in the multi-million-dollar range with a wide variety of government and private organizations.

Deeply committed to serving those who serve our country, Bill serves as President of the Board at Fort Care (fortcare.org), working year-round on an ongoing basis to feed military families through daily Honor Package deliveries on base and 4 to 6 major grocery giveaways per year—providing meals to 50,000 families last year alone.
